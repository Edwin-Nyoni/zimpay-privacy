LIVE 5 SEP 2026 v1.8.8+47

Better transaction reading

Card refunds are clearer, and missed-format reports are more useful.

ZivaPay+ now reads another EcoCash card-refund format, helping refunds reduce spending correctly instead of being missed. Format Radar reports are also easier to read, so unsupported SMS formats can be improved faster.

01

EcoCash card refunds

A newly reported card-refund SMS shape is now recognised as returned money, not ordinary income.

02

Cleaner Format Radar reports

Reports keep more of the message structure developers need while still masking private values before anything is sent.

03

Small release, precise fix

No new account, syncing or cloud workflow was added. The work stays focused on reading supported SMS formats more accurately.

Released on Google Play as v1.8.8+47.

LIVE 31 JUL 2026 v1.8.2+41

Reliability before reach

More complete reading. Safer reports. Fewer silent gaps.

The next release is prepared around one promise: if ZivaPay+ says it understands your money history, the underlying reading must be dependable and the diagnostic path must protect the person reporting a miss.

01

Safer missed-format reports

Numbers are masked automatically, identity masking is stronger, and the exact payload remains visible before it is shared.

02

Broader format coverage

New payroll and reversal shapes are recognised without treating returned money as ordinary income.

03

SMS completeness

Long inbox gaps are read page by page instead of relying on one fixed newest-message window.

LIVE 24 JUL 2026 v1.8.1+40

A measurable growth loop

Request Money can grow through a successful moment.

After a request is settled, ZivaPay+ can offer a simple way to share the app with the person who paid. Privacy-safe diagnostics now show whether that invitation is useful without sending names or amounts.
