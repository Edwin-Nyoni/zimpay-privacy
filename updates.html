LIVE 03 SEP 2026 v1.8.7+46

Usage signals · request learning

Measure the work without reading the money.

Available on Google Play. This release adds privacy-safe measurement so future ZivaPay+ improvements can be guided by real app usage, not guesses.

01

Screen reach is measurable

ZivaPay+ can now measure which main screens are opened — Home, Transactions, Analytics, Settings, Requests, Health and Recap — without sending amounts, balances, names or message content.

02

Expired requests are easier to understand

When a request expires, ZivaPay+ records only whether a possible matching payment existed locally. It does not send transaction IDs, names, amounts or settle anything automatically.

03

Sharing can be attributed safely

Generic invites and post-payment shares now include attribution links so we can learn which sharing moments help. Payment requests themselves remain linkless to keep payer trust first.

Available to everyone on Google Play since 3 September 2026.

LIVE 22 AUG 2026 v1.8.6+45

Statement accuracy · wallet evidence

A clearer record, from screen to statement.

Available on Google Play. This release makes spending breakdowns and exported statements agree more closely with the wallet activity ZivaPay+ recorded.

01

Spending stays spending

Money received is excluded from spending categories, while reversals reduce the spending they offset. The same calculation now powers Analytics, PDF and Excel exports.

02

Balances stay with their wallet

Combined statements show EcoCash and InnBucks opening and closing balances separately. If an opening balance was not recorded, the statement says “Not recorded” instead of printing $0.00.

03

Evidence is handled more carefully

Future-dated balance evidence can no longer pin the balance shown today, and an additional EcoCash “was sent to” message format is now supported.

Available to everyone on Google Play since 22 August 2026.

LIVE 15 AUG 2026 v1.8.5+44

Useful reports · privacy by preview

Keep the clue. Hide the details.

Available on Google Play. This focused release improves the voluntary report shown when ZivaPay+ cannot safely read an EcoCash or InnBucks message.

01

Direction words remain useful

Wording such as “has sent” and “to you” stays readable, giving us the structure needed to support more wallet formats.

02

Private details remain masked

Names, phone numbers, amounts, balances and reference digits are removed. You see the exact payload before deciding whether to send it.

03

A smaller app bundle

The Android App Bundle is about 10% smaller than the previous release, reducing the data needed to deliver the app. Actual Google Play download size varies by device.

Available to everyone on Google Play since 15 August 2026.

LIVE 14 AUG 2026 v1.8.4+43

Radar reliability · future readiness

See the miss sooner. Build the year on honest evidence.

This release makes supported wallet messages that ZivaPay+ could not read visible on the same Home load, while beginning private, on-device coverage groundwork for a future yearly review.

01

Misses appear without a second visit

When an EcoCash or InnBucks message cannot be read safely, the review card now appears as soon as that scan becomes visible.

02

Coverage evidence starts earlier

ZivaPay+ begins recording how far its on-device inbox evidence reaches. The future yearly review itself is not enabled in this release.

Available on Google Play since 15 August 2026.

LIVE 08 AUG 2026 v1.8.3+42

Privacy and reliability

Protect more. Guess less. Keep the record honest.

This release strengthens the voluntary reporting path, handles uncertain wallet messages more carefully, and makes the moments around requesting and receiving money clearer.

01

Stronger report privacy

Names and unknown sender identities are masked before a missed-format report can leave the phone.

02

More honest reconciliation

Ambiguous InnBucks transfers stay unclassified instead of being guessed as spending, income or an own-account transfer.

03

Clearer payment requests

Shared requests better explain ZivaPay+, with a clearer follow-up after payment is confirmed.

04

Reliability underneath

Alerts, temporary export cleanup and release-resource checks have been strengthened.

Available on Google Play at 100% rollout since 8 August 2026.
