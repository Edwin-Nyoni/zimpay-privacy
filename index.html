FAQ
Your questions, answered
Is ZivaPay+ free?

Yes — completely free to download and use on Google Play. No subscription, no hidden fees.

Can ZivaPay+ see or move my money?

No. ZivaPay+ has no bank or wallet login and cannot send, receive, or touch your money. It only organises your transaction history so you can see it clearly — it's a record-keeping tool, not a wallet.

Is my information private? Where is it kept?

Your transaction history stays on your phone — it's never uploaded to a ZivaPay+ account or synced to the cloud. We use anonymous diagnostics to fix crashes and improve the app, but never your balances or transaction details.

Why does ZivaPay+ ask for access?

EcoCash and InnBucks send you a confirmation for every transaction. ZivaPay+ uses only those money confirmations to build your history automatically — so there's no manual entry. Everything else on your phone is ignored.

Which wallets does it work with?

EcoCash and InnBucks today, with more on the way — both organised together in one place.

Do I need to create an account?

No accounts, no sign-up, no passwords. Install it and your money picture appears.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. ZivaPay+ organises and shows everything on your device, so it works even with no data.
