Straight answers

Know exactly what you are installing.

No vague banking language. ZivaPay+ is a private record-keeping and money-understanding tool, not a wallet.

Can ZivaPay+ see or move my money?

No. ZivaPay+ has no bank or wallet login and cannot send, receive or touch your money. It organises supported transaction messages already on your phone.

Why does it ask to read messages?

EcoCash and InnBucks send transaction confirmations by SMS. ZivaPay+ uses supported transaction formats to build your history automatically. Other messages are not added to your ledger.

Does my transaction history leave my phone?

Your detailed transaction history is processed and stored on your device. Limited crash and usage diagnostics help improve reliability, but exclude SMS bodies, amounts, balances, names and phone numbers.

Which wallets work today?

EcoCash and InnBucks. New formats and wallets are added only when they can be supported accurately and safely.

Is Financial Health a credit score?

No. It is a private, plain-language reading of patterns in your own on-device history. It does not approve loans, judge eligibility or report to a credit bureau.

Is ZivaPay+ free?

Yes. ZivaPay+ is currently free to download and use on Android, with no subscription and no ads.
